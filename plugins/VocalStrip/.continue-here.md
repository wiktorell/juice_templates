**UI Mockup v1:** Complete
- Aesthetic: vintage-hardware-001
- Layout: horizontal channel strip, 4 sections x 280px, 1120x420px
- EQ: 8 knobs (3 rows), HP + 2x low shelf + 3x mid + 2x high shelf

- Delay: mode toggle (Slapback/Sync), Time/Feedback/Mix knobs, Reverb Send slider
- Reverb: type toggle (Plate/Room), Pre-Delay/Decay/Damping/Mix knobs
